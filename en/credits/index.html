Jump motion data

The motion of the 3D jump models comes from the motion-capture dataset “FS-Jump3D”, converted into movements of the models’ skeletons.

Ryota Tanaka, Tomohiro Suzuki, Keisuke Fujii. "3D Pose-Based Temporal Action Segmentation for Figure Skating: A Fine-Grained and Jump Procedure-Aware Annotation Approach." MMSports '24.
